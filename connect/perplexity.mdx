---
title: "Connect Perplexity"
description: "Add AdLoop Cloud to Perplexity as a custom remote connector."
---

<Info>
Custom connectors need a **Pro, Max or Enterprise** plan. On Enterprise, an admin first turns on *Allow members to add custom connectors* under Enterprise settings → Connectors; it's off by default (checked 2026-10-10).
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a custom connector">
    In Perplexity: **Account settings → Connectors → + Custom connector** and choose **Remote**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the server">
    Name: `AdLoop`. MCP Server URL:

    ```
    https://mcp.getadloop.com/mcp
    ```

    Authentication: **OAuth**. Transport: **Streamable HTTP**. Leave client ID and secret empty; Perplexity registers itself with AdLoop. Tick the acknowledgement and click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize">
    Click the AdLoop card to sign in. AdLoop's consent page opens; sign in to your workspace and choose **Allow access**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it">
    In the composer, turn AdLoop on in the connector picker (or type `@`), and make sure the search focus isn't set to web only. Then ask: *"Run the AdLoop health check."*
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After an AdLoop update

Perplexity remembers AdLoop's tool list from when you connected it. When AdLoop adds tools, they only show up after a refresh: remove the AdLoop connector under **Account settings → Connectors** and add it again; Perplexity has no refresh button for custom connectors.

Not sure whether yours is current? Ask: *"Run the AdLoop health check."* It lists the tools AdLoop offers you and says so when your app is showing an older list.

## Notes

- Enterprise admins can share the connector with the whole organization and choose whether members sign in individually.
- Every write goes through the [preview and confirm flow](/concepts/safety-model): Perplexity can draft a change, but nothing is applied until you confirm it.

</Tip>
