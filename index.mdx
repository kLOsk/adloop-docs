AdLoop connects your Google marketing stack to the AI assistant you already use: Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or Gemini. Analyze campaigns, hunt wasted spend, build ads by chat. Every change is previewed and dry-run first; nothing goes live without your approval.

Both editions run the same tools with the same safety model. The difference is who handles the plumbing.

## What you can do

- **Analyze**: campaign performance, search terms, quality scores, PMax insights, cross-referenced Ads and GA4 data that answers "what's my real cost per signup?"
- **Build**: complete campaigns by chat. Keywords, ads, sitelinks, and budgets drafted as previews, created paused, enabled only by you.
- **Clean up**: wasted spend hunts, negative keyword workflows, three-way tracking audits across your website code, GTM container, and GA4.
- **Stay safe**: previews with plan IDs, dry-run by default, budget caps, an audit log, and read-only keys. See [the safety model](/concepts/safety-model).
