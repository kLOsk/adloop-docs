A slow landing page lowers Quality Score, raises click costs and loses visitors before the page has rendered. `analyze_page_speed` runs Google PageSpeed Insights on a URL and returns two views of it: a **Lighthouse** lab test, and **field data** from real Chrome users (the Chrome UX Report) where Google has enough of it.

No Google account is involved: PageSpeed Insights fetches the page itself, so there is nothing to connect.

## What you get

- **Performance score** from 0 to 100.
- **Lab metrics:** Largest Contentful Paint, Cumulative Layout Shift, Total Blocking Time, First Contentful Paint.
- **Field metrics** over the last 28 days: the 75th percentile for LCP, Interaction to Next Paint and CLS, each rated fast, average or slow, plus an overall rating.
- **Top opportunities:** the largest Lighthouse improvements with their estimated savings.
- **Insights** that flag a score below 50, a slow real-user rating, or an LCP above the 2.5 second threshold.

## Behaviour worth knowing

- **Mobile by default**, because that's where most paid traffic lands; pass `strategy="desktop"` for the other view.
- **A run takes 10 to 30 seconds.** That's Lighthouse loading the page, not a hang.
- **Field data beats lab data.** Lab numbers come from a simulated load. When the two disagree, trust the real-user numbers. No field data means the page has too little Chrome traffic, not that it's fast.
- **Public pages only.** Google fetches the URL from its own servers, so localhost, staging behind a login or pages blocked by a firewall can't be measured.
- It pairs with [`landing_page_analysis`](/integrations/tracking-audit#tools): a page with paid traffic, no conversions and a poor score needs speed work before new ad copy.

## Tools

- `analyze_page_speed`: PageSpeed Insights for one URL

Parameters are in the [tool reference](/reference/tools#web-performance-web); the toolset slug is `web`.

## Limits and storage

On AdLoop Cloud, PageSpeed calls are unmetered on every plan; see [plans and limits](/cloud/plans-and-limits). AdLoop stores nothing for this tool: the URL and the results pass through.
