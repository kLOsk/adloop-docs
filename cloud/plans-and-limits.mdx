AdLoop Cloud measures one thing: **ad platform operations**, the calls your AI makes to Google Ads and Reddit Ads. Everything else (Analytics, Tag Manager, Search Console, Merchant Center, PageSpeed) is unmetered on every plan, including Free.

## Plans

| | Free | Pro | Agency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ad platform operations per month | 250 | No limit | No limit |
| Ad platform operations per day | 100 | No limit in normal use (fair use: 2,000) | No limit in normal use (fair use: 5,000) |
| Analytics, Tag Manager, Search Console, Merchant Center, PageSpeed | Unmetered | Unmetered | Unmetered |
| Accounts | One profile: one ad account per platform + 1 GA4 property | One profile: one ad account per platform + 1 GA4 property | Unlimited [client profiles](/cloud/clients) |
| Writes with preview, dry run and budget cap | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| API keys | Yes | Yes | Also scoped to single clients |
| Credit card | Not needed | Required | Required |

Free has no time limit. Prices are on the [pricing page](https://getadloop.com/pricing); you book or change a plan under **Settings → Billing**.

The fair-use ceilings on Pro and Agency sit far above anything a real workspace has used. They exist so a runaway script stops somewhere.

## What counts as an operation

Every Google Ads or Reddit Ads tool call is one operation, reads and drafts alike. The two platforms count together, against the daily number and, on Free, the monthly one.

- **`confirm_and_apply`**, and a click on the [approval card](/concepts/safety-model#approval-cards-only-your-click-applies), counts toward the platform of the plan it applies. Applying a GA4 key event doesn't count.
- **Cross-channel tools** that query Google Ads (`analyze_campaign_conversions`, `landing_page_analysis`, `attribution_check`) count as Google Ads operations.
- **Not counted:** `health_check` and `list_accounts`, so checking that everything works never costs you operations.
- **Refused calls aren't counted.** A call that stops before reaching the ad platform (setup not finished, a read-only key, a toolset the key doesn't include, the limit itself) shows up as activity but costs nothing.

## The limits

- **Daily:** resets at **midnight UTC**.
- **Monthly (Free only):** 250 operations per calendar month, reset on the **1st (UTC)**. It comes on top of the daily limit, so a single day still stops at 100.

The dashboard shows today's usage and, on Free, this month's. You get an email when a workspace passes 80% of a limit (once per day for the daily one, once per month for the monthly one), so the limit is never the first sign.

At a limit, Google Ads and Reddit Ads tools are refused with a message that says which limit was reached and when it resets. Analytics, Tag Manager, Search Console, Merchant Center and PageSpeed keep working, and so do `health_check` and `list_accounts`.

The daily plan limits apply to every workspace since 15 October 2026. The monthly Free limit applies to workspaces created since 20 October 2026, and to every free workspace from 1 December 2026.
