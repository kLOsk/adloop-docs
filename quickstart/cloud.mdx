title: "Quickstart: AdLoop Cloud"
description: "From registration to your first analysis in under five minutes."
---

AdLoop Cloud hosts everything for you: Google API access, OAuth, credential encryption, and the MCP endpoint at `mcp.getadloop.com`. You bring a Google account with access to your Ads account or GA4 property, and an AI tool that speaks MCP.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Register at [getadloop.com](https://getadloop.com/register). The free plan covers one Ads account and one GA4 property and needs no credit card. [Pricing](https://getadloop.com/pricing) if you need more, and [plans and limits](/cloud/plans-and-limits) for what each plan includes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your ad platforms">
    The onboarding wizard asks which platforms you use: Google, Reddit, or both. You then sign in to each one in turn and pick which accounts your AI may see: for Google an Ads account, a GA4 property, and optionally a Tag Manager container, Search Console property, or Merchant Center account; for Reddit an ad account.

    You can skip a platform in the wizard and connect it later from the sidebar (**Settings → Google** or **Settings → Reddit**).

    <Note>
    AdLoop Cloud is verified by Google, so you see Google's normal consent dialog with no warning screen. You pick there which services to grant, AdLoop never sees your password, and you can revoke access in your Google account at any time.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create an API key">
    The wizard creates your personal key automatically. It's shown once and stored encrypted; you can reveal, restrict, or revoke keys later under **Settings → API keys**. Optional restrictions: [read-only](/concepts/safety-model#read-only-keys) and [toolsets](/concepts/toolsets).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your AI">
    Follow the guide for your tool: [claude.ai](/connect/claude-ai), [ChatGPT](/connect/chatgpt), [Claude Code](/connect/claude-code), [Cursor](/connect/cursor), or [any other client](/connect/overview): Codex, VS Code, Gemini, Mistral Vibe, Perplexity, Zed and more. The dashboard's **Connect AI** page walks you through each of these, with your key already filled in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask something real">
    Try: *"How did my campaigns perform in the last 30 days, and where am I wasting spend?"*
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The order matters

Connecting an ad platform (Google or Reddit) comes before API keys, and API keys before connecting your AI. A key created before any account is selected would only ever return setup errors, so the dashboard enforces the sequence.
